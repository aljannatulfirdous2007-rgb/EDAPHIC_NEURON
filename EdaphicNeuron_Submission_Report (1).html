Layer 1 — Dynamic Prioritization

1
District Biological Risk Scorer
Your Model — M1

What It Does

  • Fuses NPSS pest severity + IMD humidity/temperature + Farmonaut NDVI into one district risk score (0-100)
  • Score above 65 triggers urgent field response — rep's entire day changes automatically
  • Crop stage vulnerability acts as biological amplifier — peak vulnerability = 1.5× score
  • Pest-specific temperature thresholds validated by agri domain expertise

Gap It Fills

  • Every competitor uses sales targets and geography. Zero biological intelligence. This is the first system that uses crop emergency as the primary driver.
Algorithm
Weighted Fusion + Logistic Regression
Domain-validated weights (pest 40% + humidity 25% + temperature 20% + NDVI 15%) beat black-box models on interpretability
Data Sources
NPSS · IMD API · Farmonaut · KDSS
All public, government-grade, available today
Response Threshold
≥65 = Urgent (4hr) · ≥40 = Medium (24hr)
Calibrated against historical outbreak severity data
2
Retailer Urgency Ranker
Your Model — M2

What It Does

  • XGBoost model scores every retailer daily by predicted sale probability under current biological conditions
  • Inputs: district risk score + inventory level + days since visit + farmer count + competitor status
  • Feature engineering captures the "perfect storm" — risk × low inventory × high farmer count
  • List reranks in real time when new pest alert fires

Why XGBoost

  • Finds non-obvious interaction effects. A retailer with medium risk + very low inventory + 400 farmers + competitor out of stock = higher urgency than high risk alone. No human writes that rule.
Algorithm
XGBoost Classifier + SHAP
Best tabular data classifier. Native SHAP support for explainability built in.
Key Features
urgency_interaction · competitor_opportunity · recency_urgency
Engineered features that capture compound risk scenarios
Training Data
Syngenta synthetic CRM + POS dataset
Target: sale_made (binary). AUC target >0.78
3
Route Optimizer
Your Model — M3

What It Does

  • Takes urgency-ranked retailer list and finds optimal visit sequence
  • Uses urgency-weighted distance — urgent retailers appear "closer" in the optimization
  • Recalculates in milliseconds when new pest alert fires mid-morning
  • Returns estimated arrival times for each stop with driving distance summary
Algorithm
Nearest Neighbor + 2-Opt Improvement
Full TSP is NP-hard. 2-Opt delivers 95% of optimal in milliseconds. Fast enough for live demo recalculation.
Urgency Multiplier
Rank 1 = 0.3× distance · Rank 8 = 1.5× distance
Makes urgent retailers appear geographically attractive regardless of physical distance
4
Crop-Stage Visit Filter
Rule-Based

What It Does

  • Checks KDSS crop calendar before every visit recommendation
  • Suppresses visits when product is not agronomically relevant for current growth stage
  • Cotton at seedling stage → bollworm insecticide visit suppressed automatically
  • Eliminates Syngenta's stated problem: "marketing fungicide when there is no disease pressure"
Data Source
KDSS Crop Calendar · AgriStack District Data
Direct Problem Solved
Wasted visits eliminated at source

6
SHAP Explainability Layer
Your Model — M7

Why This Is The Most Important Feature

  • A rep who doesn't understand a recommendation ignores it — this is why acceptance rates fail
  • SHAP calculates exact contribution of each factor: Pest Risk 45% + Inventory 30% + Recency 15% + Farmers 10%
  • Translated by Claude into plain Hinglish the rep understands and can repeat to the retailer
  • Displayed as a horizontal bar chart on the action card — rep sees it before walking in
  • Syngenta's brief explicitly requires explainable recommendations — this delivers it mathematically
Algorithm
SHAP TreeExplainer (Shapley Values)
Industry standard for ML explainability. Mathematically rigorous. Wraps XGBoost natively.
No Other Team Has This

Layer 4 — Outcome Learning Loop

11
Contextual Bandit Outcome Learner
Her Model — M6

What Makes This Extraordinary

  • System in Week 8 is dramatically better than Week 1 — without anyone touching the code
  • Every visit outcome (sale/order/no purchase) updates routing priority weights automatically

Section Six
Limitations & Assumptions
06
Honest acknowledgement

What We Know We Don't Know.

Judges who work in agriculture for 20 years will respect honest limitations more than oversold promises. We acknowledge these directly.

Assumptions

📱
Rep Device Baseline
We assume reps have Android smartphones with minimum 2GB RAM for PWA functionality. Feature phone reps receive SMS-based simplified daily plan as fallback.
📊
Dataset Outcome History
M2 XGBoost requires at least 6 months of rep visit logs with outcomes to train above AUC 0.75. Syngenta's synthetic dataset is assumed to contain this.
🗺️
Territory Data
Retailer catchment zones and farmer-retailer linkages are assumed available in Syngenta's CRM or approximatable from AgriStack + POS data for pilot districts.
🌾
NPSS Granularity
NPSS operates at district level. Block-level precision would significantly improve targeting. We use NDVI stress zones as a sub-district proxy where needed.

Known Limitations

❄️
Cold Start Problem
Bandit model (M6) requires outcome history to improve. New districts use rule-based recommendations until 30 days of real outcomes accumulate.
⏱️
NPSS Data Latency
NPSS bulletins can lag field reality by 24-48 hours. M4 early pest predictor and M5 anomaly detector provide early warning that partially compensates.
🔄
Offline Sync Edge Cases
If two reps visit the same retailer offline and log conflicting data, last-write-wins with a manager alert. Estimated 2-3% of sync events will require manual resolution.
📐
Prototype Scope
Current prototype covers cotton and rice in 2 pilot districts with mock data. Full national rollout requires expanded crop-pest mappings and carrier-grade PWA infrastructure.
